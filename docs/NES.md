# NES References

NES Console uses an Master Clock that is an product of the Color subcarrier frequency divided by 6.

The Master clock later on is subdivided into their respective components.

|  | NTSC (2A03 NES) | PAL (2A07 NES) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Color subcarrier frequency | 3.579545 MHz | 4.433619 MHz |
| Master clock frequency | 21.477272 MHz | 26.601712 MHz |
